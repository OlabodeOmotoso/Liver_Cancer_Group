# Liver Cancer Genetics Group Training

Hands-on training in the command line, R and GWAS, with a focus on the genetics of liver cancer in African and African-ancestry populations.

Nigerian Liver Cancer Molecular Research Group.

**Format:** 12 sessions, twice a month, 90 minutes each.

## Getting the course files

1. Go to the repository: <https://github.com/OlabodeOmotoso/Liver_Cancer_Group>
2. Click **Code** then **Download ZIP**, or clone it with `git clone`.
3. Unzip into a folder you can find easily, for example `Documents/Liver_Cancer_Group`.

## Software used

| Purpose | Tool |
|---|---|
| Chromatogram viewing | FinchTV (backup: SnapGene Viewer or 4Peaks) |
| Sequence search | NCBI BLAST |
| Translation | Expasy Translate |
| Later sessions | R, RStudio, PLINK, ADMIXTURE |
